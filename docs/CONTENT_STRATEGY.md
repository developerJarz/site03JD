### Fixed in this pass
- **Blog headings:** every post repeated its title as a sub-heading and used h3/h4 with no h2. All 8 now use a proper H1 → H2 → H3 outline, which also switches on the “On this page” contents list.
- **Blog titles:** three titles had lost their colons in the WordPress export (e.g. “…Business Profile A Step-by-Step Guide”).
- **Linking:**
  - every post now ends with a box for the service it’s about, and its closing banner points to that service;
  - every service page now lists its related guides, plus “[Service] in Dallas / Denver / Calgary / Dhaka” links to the location pages.
- **SEO titles and descriptions** for all 63 public pages now pass the audit in Admin → SEO → Page SEO.

### Problems that need your decision
1. **Two posts compete for the same search.** “Why Small Business Owners Should Invest in Our Monthly Business Management Package” and “Why Our Full Business Management Service Package is Essential…” cover the same ground, with 7 near-identical numbered points. Recommendation: merge them into one stronger post and redirect the other URL to it (301).
2. **Three posts overlap on “what is local SEO”:**
   - “Why Website Local SEO is Important”
   - “Why Local SEO is a Game-Changer for Businesses in Dallas”
   - “Why Map Ranking is Crucial”

   Give each a distinct job:
   - make the first the **evergreen “What is local SEO?” guide** (the hub article);
   - keep the Dallas post **Dallas-specific** (local examples, Dallas searches);
   - narrow the map-ranking post to the **Google Map Pack only**.
3. **No named author.** Posts are credited to “Jarz Digital”. Google rewards content with visible expertise (its E-E-A-T guidelines), and you have a strong author in your founder: 12 years in SEO, the #1 Local SEO freelancer on Fiverr in 2023, and more than 300 businesses ranked. With his approval, credit the SEO posts to him and link to his team page.
4. **Bangladesh projects are marked “needs review”.** Confirm the descriptions with each client before turning them into case studies.

## 4. Briefs for the first five

### 1 · Google Business Profile optimization checklist
- **Target:** “Google Business Profile optimization”, “GBP checklist”, “optimize Google My Business”
- **Title options:**
  - The Google Business Profile Optimization Checklist (2026)

  - How to Optimize Your Google Business Profile: A Complete Checklist

  - Why the profile drives Map Pack visibility

  - Reviews
  - Q&A
  - Products and services

  - Monthly upkeep

  - send new posts to the footer newsletter list (already collecting sign-ups; the plan is one email per post);
  - offer the GBP checklist as a download in exchange for an email address.

## 7. How to measure

| What | Where | Target after 90 days |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions and clicks per post | Google Search Console → Pages | Each new post indexed and getting impressions within 4 weeks |
| Queries per location page | Search Console → Queries, filtered by `/locations/` | `/locations/dhaka` appearing for “… Dhaka” searches |
| Leads from content | Admin → Leads (source) | At least 1 lead a month traced to a blog or location page |
| On-page health | Admin → SEO → Page SEO | 0 pages “Need attention” |

Review each quarter:
- rewrite posts that get impressions but few clicks (usually the title or description);
- expand posts ranking in positions 8–20;
- pick the next quarter’s topics from the searches that actually brought visitors.
